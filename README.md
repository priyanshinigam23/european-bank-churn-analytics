## Internship Project

An interactive data analytics dashboard designed to explore customer churn patterns and customer segments in a European banking dataset.

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn across geographical, demographic, and account-related characteristics. It uses Python-based data analysis and interactive visualizations to identify customer segments that may require further investigation.

## Objectives

- Analyse overall customer churn and retention.
- Compare churn rates across countries.
- Explore churn patterns by age group and gender.
- Investigate account activity, credit scores, tenure, balances, and product ownership.
- Present findings through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

## Dataset

- Customer records: 10,000
- Original attributes: 14
- Study period: 2025
- Target variable: `Exited` (1 = churned, 0 = retained)

## Key Findings

- Overall churn rate: 20.37%
- Germany recorded the highest observed country-level churn rate: 32.44%.
- Customers aged 46–60 recorded a churn rate of 51.12%.
- Inactive customers recorded a churn rate of 26.85%, compared with 14.27% among active customers.
- The high-balance segment recorded a churn rate of 25.23%.

These are descriptive findings from the analysed dataset, not proof of causation or predictions of future churn.

## Limitations

The dataset covers 2025 only. This project uses descriptive analysis and does not include a validated predictive model. Results may not represent the entire European banking industry.
